(01) / THE PERSON

A little about me.

I'm a software engineer in the making, fascinated by the space where technology, people, and ideas meet.

I'm studying Computer Science at the University of Michigan with a minor in Philosophy. I love figuring out how things work, but care just as much about whether what we're building makes someone's day—or the world—a little better.

I've worked on everything from production-floor tools and client websites to machine learning models and autonomous vehicles. Different settings, same favorite challenge: understand the problem, make something useful, and keep improving it.

My interests in applied ethics and global priorities continually shape how I think about my responsibilities as an engineer and the impact of the technology I help create.
